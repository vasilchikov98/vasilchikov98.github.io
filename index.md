# health-pet

A personal health analytics tool. It has one user: its owner.

## Privacy Policy

- health-pet reads only the owner's own Fitbit data from the Google Health API: sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, activity and workouts. Access is read-only.
- - The data is stored only in the owner's private storage: a private GitHub repository and the owner's Notion workspace.
  - - The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
    - - No data of any other person is collected.
      - - Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account: Security, Third-party apps, health-pet.
